Camera

Your camera, background effects and how you see other people's cameras. Camera sharing needs a server with a mumpeer relay.

Camera

You
Preview, only on this device. It stops when you leave this page.

Auto (up to 720p · 30 fps) sends only as much as the people watching need: smaller in a big grid, sharper when someone pins you.

Only your own view is mirrored. Others see you the right way round.

Background

Auto uses the graphics card when it can and switches to Light on battery or when the PC is busy.

Your own background images stay on this device (2 saved). Only the finished picture is sent. Manage images

Privacy

Your camera always starts off and shows a preview first. It turns off when you leave the channel or disconnect. Only people in your voice channel can see it, through this server's relay, end-to-end encrypted. Mumcord never records cameras.

Other people's cameras

Always opens the camera room when someone in your channel turns on a camera. Never keeps you voice-only and downloads no video.

Follows your system's reduce-motion setting by default.
